The core transmission dynamics are described by an SEIR-type model with two stages for both the Exposed (E) and Infectious (I) compartments (SE₁E₂I₁I₂R). The infection process is modeled as a `ContactFlow` from the Susceptible compartment, driven by the combined population of both Infectious compartments (I₁ and I₂). This is implemented using two separate `ContactFlow`s, one for each infectious source, which is the standard method for representing summed contact sources within the metamodel. All subsequent transitions (progression through latent and infectious stages, and final recovery) are modeled as `RateFlow`s.
