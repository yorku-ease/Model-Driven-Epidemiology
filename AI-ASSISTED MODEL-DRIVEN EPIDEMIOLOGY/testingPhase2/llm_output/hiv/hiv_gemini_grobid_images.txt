An epidemiological model for HIV transmission was extracted from the provided scientific paper sections and figure descriptions. The model is a deterministic compartmental model described by a system of ordinary differential equations. It stratifies the population into three susceptible groups based on sexual behavior and gender: homosexual men, heterosexual men, and women. The model tracks the progression from susceptible to infected, then to treatment (ART), and finally to AIDS.

Key features of the model include:
- **Population Stratification**: The model explicitly separates susceptible and infected populations into Homosexual Men (Sh, Ih), Women (Sw, Iw), and Heterosexual Men (Sm, Im).
- **Complex Transmission**: Infection is driven by forces of infection (λh, λw, λm) that account for exclusive homosexual, exclusive heterosexual, and bisexual contact between the groups.
- **Treatment and Progression**: Infected individuals may start treatment (T) or progress directly to AIDS (A). Those on treatment can also progress to AIDS due to treatment failure.
- **Demographics**: The model includes constant recruitment into the susceptible populations and accounts for both natural mortality in all stages and AIDS-induced mortality.
