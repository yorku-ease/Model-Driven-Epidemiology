An SE₂(2)IR (Susceptible, Exposed x2, Infectious x2, Recovered) compartmental model was extracted. The model structure is detailed in the "Transmission model" section, specifying two sequential exposed compartments (E₁, E₂) and two sequential infectious compartments (I₁, I₂). This structure models Erlang-distributed sojourn times for the latent and infectious periods.

Parameter values were primarily sourced from "Table 1" (known parameters) and "Table 2" (posterior median estimates for the 2014/15 season) in the provided tables.
- **Compartments:** S, E₁, E₂, I₁, I₂, and R.
- **Initial Conditions:** Based on the 2014/15 season, the initial susceptible proportion (`π`) and initial number of infectious individuals (`I₀`) were used to populate the compartments.
- **Transmission:** Infection is a `ContactFlow` from Susceptible to Exposed, driven by contact with both infectious compartments (I₁ and I₂) at a rate of `β`. The value for `β` was derived from the reported Basic Reproduction Number (`R₀ = 2.145`) and recovery rate (`γ`) for an SE₂(2)IR model using the formula `β = R₀ * γ`.
- **Progression & Recovery:** Transitions through the latent (`E₁ → E₂ → I₁`) and infectious (`I₁ → I₂ → R`) stages are modeled as `RateFlows` with rates `σ` and `γ` respectively.
