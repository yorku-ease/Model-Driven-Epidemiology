An SE₂I₂R (Susceptible, Exposed-1, Exposed-2, Infectious-1, Infectious-2, Recovered) model is described. The use of two sequential compartments for both the Exposed and Infectious stages models Erlang-distributed sojourn times, which is a common technique to create more realistic, less exponentially-distributed waiting times for incubation and infectious periods.

The model dynamics are as follows:
*   **Compartments**: The model consists of six compartments: Susceptible (S), two stages of Exposed (E₁, E₂), two stages of Infectious (I₁, I₂), and Recovered (R).
*   **Parameters**: The model is governed by three key parameters:
    *   **β (beta)**: The transmission rate, representing the probability of infection upon contact between a susceptible and an infectious individual.
    *   **σ (sigma)**: The rate of progression through the exposed (latent) stages. The total average incubation period is 2/σ.
    *   **γ (gamma)**: The rate of progression through the infectious stages, which is equivalent to the recovery rate. The total average infectious period is 2/γ.
*   **Flows**:
    *   Susceptible individuals become exposed through contact with infectious individuals from either the I₁ or I₂ compartment at a rate determined by β. This is a `ContactFlow`.
    *   Exposed individuals progress from E₁ to E₂ and then from E₂ to I₁ at a rate σ.
    *   Infectious individuals progress from I₁ to I₂ and then from I₂ to the Recovered state at a rate γ.
    *   These progression flows are all `RateFlow`s, as they depend only on the population of the source compartment.

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<seir:SEIRModel xmi:version="2.0" xmlns:xmi="http://www.omg.org/XMI" xmlns:xsi="http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema-instance" xmlns:seir="http://example.com/seirmodel">
  <parameters name="σ" description="Progression rate through exposed stages. The average incubation period is 2/σ." unit="1/day"/>
  <parameters name="γ" description="Progression rate through infectious stages (recovery rate). The average infectious period is 2/γ." unit="1/day"/>
  <parameters name="β" description="Transmission rate per contact between a susceptible and an infectious individual." unit="1/day"/>
  <compartments PrimaryName="Susceptible">
    <outgoingFlows xsi:type="seir:ContactFlow" contactRateParameter="//@parameters.2" contactCompartment="//@compartments.3" target="//@compartments.1" description="Infection from contact with Infectious 1 individuals."/>
    <outgoingFlows xsi:type="seir:ContactFlow" contactRateParameter="//@parameters.2" contactCompartment="//@compartments.4" target="//@compartments.1" description="Infection from contact with Infectious 2 individuals."/>
  </compartments>
  <compartments PrimaryName="Exposed" SecondaryName="1">
    <outgoingFlows xsi:type="seir:RateFlow" rateParameter="//@parameters.0" target="//@compartments.2" description="Progression from first to second exposed stage."/>
  </compartments>
  <compartments PrimaryName="Exposed" SecondaryName="2">
    <outgoingFlows xsi:type="seir:RateFlow" rateParameter="//@parameters.0" target="//@compartments.3" description="Progression from second exposed stage to infectious (onset of infectiousness)."/>
  </compartments>
  <compartments PrimaryName="Infectious" SecondaryName="1">
    <outgoingFlows xsi:type="seir:RateFlow" rateParameter="//@parameters.1" target="//@compartments.4" description="Progression from first to second infectious stage."/>
  </compartments>
  <compartments PrimaryName="Infectious" SecondaryName="2">
    <outgoingFlows xsi:type="seir:RateFlow" rateParameter="//@parameters.1" target="//@compartments.5" description="Recovery from the second infectious stage."/>
  </compartments>
  <compartments PrimaryName="Recovered"/>
</seir:SEIRModel>
