- **Parameters**: Key parameters are extracted from Table 1 and the model equations. Vaccinated individuals can lose their immunity and return to the Susceptible state. Infected individuals recover and move to the Recovered state. Recovered individuals can either lose their natural immunity and return to Susceptible or become reinfected and move back to the Infected state. All compartments experience a natural death rate.
