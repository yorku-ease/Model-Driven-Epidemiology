An epidemiological model for Zika virus has been extracted from the provided scientific paper sections, parameter tables, and figure descriptions. The model follows a vector-host structure.

- **Human Population:** Modeled using an SEIR (Susceptible, Exposed, Infectious, Recovered) framework.
- **Vector Population:** Modeled with a full life-cycle approach, including compartments for Eggs (V₁), Larvae (V₂), and Pupae (V₃), which mature into an adult population following an SEI (Susceptible, Exposed, Infectious) framework.
- **Transmission:** Occurs through contact between infectious vectors and susceptible humans, and between infectious humans and susceptible vectors. The transmission rate is frequency-dependent, influenced by the total human population and the presence of alternative animal hosts.
- **Population Dynamics:** The vector population is dynamic, with a birth rate corresponding to egg-laying by adult female mosquitoes and stage-specific mortality rates. For simplicity and adherence to the metamodel's capabilities, non-linear dynamics such as human logistic growth and larval-stage crowding mortality have been omitted, as suggested as a possible simplification in the paper's discussion.
