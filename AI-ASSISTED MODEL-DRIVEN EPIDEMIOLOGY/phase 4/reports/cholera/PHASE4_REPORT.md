## Sensitivity analysis

**Most influential parameters:**
- ξ
- β
- 1/γ
- 1/δ
- κ

- **Tornado plot:** see `sensitivity_tornado.png`

Here the sensitivity analysis says that ξ (the contamination-related parameter in the benchmark) dominates the uncertainty in outcomes, with β and the other rates having smaller effects within the tested range. In other words, for this cholera model, improving our knowledge of ξ would reduce output uncertainty the most.
